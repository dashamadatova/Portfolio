How to play

1

Start

Press Space or click the game to begin. The puppy starts running on its own.

2

Jump

Use Space, ↑, W, a click or a tap to jump over each stool that slides toward you.

3

Survive

Your score rises the longer you run. Touch a stool and it’s game over.

4

Try again

After a short pause, jump again to restart and beat your best score.

Game mechanics

MechanicHow it works
Running speedStarts at 250 px/s and keeps climbing as you survive.
JumpOne jump rises about 245 px and hangs in the air for roughly 1 second. You can’t jump again mid-air.
ObstaclesStools (95 × 105 px) spawn off-screen on the right and slide left toward the puppy.
CollisionA slightly forgiving hitbox: only a real hit ends the run.
Score & bestScore grows with time survived; your best score is remembered in this browser.
AnimationThe puppy glides across the floor and leaps into the air on every jump.
